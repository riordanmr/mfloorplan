Help

Search for Items

Type in the search box to find parts and tools by name. Results appear instantly as you type.

View All Items

Click the "All" button to see a list of all parts and tools in the shop, with all locations highlighted on the map.

Browse Locations

Tap on any location box on the map to see what items are stored there.

Click Search Results

When you see search results, click on an item to highlight only its location(s) on the map.

Navigate the Map

  • Desktop: Use mouse wheel to zoom, click and drag to pan
  • Mobile: Pinch to zoom, drag with one finger to pan

Clear Results

Click the "Clear" button to remove all highlights and close the results list.
